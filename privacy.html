Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Grok Bot Health is a personal-use application operated by Wilfredo Toro for his own Google Health / Fitbit account only.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent, the app reads his own Google Health data (sleep, activity, heart rate, weight, nutrition, profile and settings) and may write nutrition or weight entries he asks it to log.

How data is used

The data is used only to produce personal health and fitness summaries and coaching for the owner. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train machine learning models.

Storage

OAuth tokens are stored on the owner's own computer. Health data is retrieved on demand and is not published anywhere.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
